Ask us directly

Do you guarantee a job?

No, and be careful of anyone who does. The hiring decision belongs to the airline and depends on their vacancies as much as on you. What we can point to is our record: more than 5,000 students placed since 1996 with domestic and international carriers. Our part is to get you genuinely ready and in front of the right openings.

Do I need any experience?

None. Most students come from school, college, retail or hospitality. You need enough English to hold a simple conversation, and the counselling session will tell you where you stand before you commit to anything.

Am I eligible?

Most airlines require you to be 18 or over and to reach roughly 212 cm, flat-footed or on tiptoe depending on the carrier. Most also expect you to swim unaided and to have no tattoos visible in uniform. Height is the one thing training cannot change, so we check it early and tell you honestly which carriers fit.

How long is the course, and what does it cost?

REPLACE THIS with the real course length, fee structure and any instalment option. It is the first thing a serious enquiry looks for.

What if I'm turned down at an assessment day?

Many people are, the first time — plenty of working crew were turned down once before they were hired. Come back and tell us which stage it was. That is the most useful thing either of us can know, and it changes what we work on before you apply again.

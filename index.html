Questions

Asked and answered honestly

Do you guarantee a job?

No — and be careful with anyone who does. The hiring decision belongs to the airline and depends on their vacancies as much as on you. What we can point to is the record: more than 5,000 students placed since 1996 with domestic and international carriers. Our part is getting you genuinely ready and putting you in front of the right openings.

Do I need any experience?

None. Most people arrive from school, college, retail or hospitality. You need enough English to hold a simple conversation — if you can read this page, you can start — and the counselling session tells you where you stand before you commit to anything.

Am I eligible? Age, height, schooling?

Most airlines require you to be 18 or over, and to reach roughly 212 cm flat-footed or on tiptoes depending on the carrier. Most also expect you to swim unaided and to have no tattoos visible in uniform. Height is the one thing training cannot change, so we check it early and tell you honestly which carriers fit.

What if I'm rejected at an assessment day?

Many people are, the first time — plenty of working crew were turned down once before they were hired. Come back and tell us which stage lost it. That is the most useful information either of us can have, and it changes what we work on before you apply again.

Before you ask

The questions worth answering honestly

Do you guarantee me a job?

Nobody can promise you a specific job — the hiring decision belongs to the airline and depends on their vacancies as much as on you. What we can point to is the record: more than 5,000 students placed since 1996, across domestic and international carriers. Our part is getting you genuinely ready, and then putting you in front of the right openings.

Do I need any experience?

None. Most people arrive from retail, teaching, nursing or straight from school, and we start from what crew actually do all day. You need enough English to hold a simple conversation — if you can read this page you can start — and the free assessment tells you where you are before you commit to anything.

Am I too old, or too short?

Most airlines require 18 or over, some 21, and a reach of 212 cm flat or on tiptoes depending on the carrier. There's no upper age limit at most airlines. Height is the one thing we can't train around, so we measure it at the free assessment and tell you which carriers fit.

How long is the course, and what does it cost?

REPLACE THIS with your real course length, fees and any instalment option. Nothing on this page currently claims a duration or a price, so this is the one answer a serious enquiry will look for and not find.

What if I'm rejected at an assessment day?

Many people are, the first time — plenty of working crew were turned down once before they were hired. Come back and tell us which stage lost it; that's the most useful information either of us can have, and it changes what we work on before you apply again.
